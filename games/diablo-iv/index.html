Soul Splinters (Jewelry Gems)
  • ✓ 7 types & 5 rarities of socketable jewelry items (upgradeable in Cube). Strong stat boosts paired with resource/defense trade-offs.
Open World & Dungeons (PTR 3.2.0 Testbed)
  • ✓ Challenge Dungeons: World portals leading to boss fights with up to +3 Torment difficulty scaling.
  • ✓ Overland Ambush: Rare high-difficulty open-world ambushes with a 30-second warning.
Uniques & Mythic Crafting Rework
  • ✓ Legacy Uniques: 9–10 iconic items from D1/D2/D3 (Leoric's Crown, Nemesis Bracers, Stone of Jordan, Squirt's Blouse, In-Geom, The Furnace, etc.).

📖 About Seasons
Diablo IV is an action RPG developed and published by Blizzard Entertainment. It returns to the dark, gothic roots of the series, featuring a vast shared open world of Sanctuary. Diablo IV operates on a quarterly seasonal model, with each season introducing new storylines, unique mechanics, class balance updates, and new Battle Pass rewards.
